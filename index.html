From Scrivener

Compile → format "Plain Text" or "MultiMarkdown", with chapter titles turned on. MultiMarkdown keeps italics as *italic*. Moss Press splits chapters at lines starting with # or at lines like "Chapter 3" or "Prologue". If your chapters start some other way, change Split chapters at before you import: short title lines, page breaks, or a marker line you choose. Anything that still lands wrong can be fixed with Split chapter at cursor and Join with next chapter under the chapter text.

Welcome to Moss Press

Moss Press turns your finished manuscript into a print-ready PDF. Here's how it works.

  1. Get your file ready. Moss Press reads plain text (.txt) and Markdown (.md); Markdown keeps your italics. From Word, use Save As, Plain Text. From Scrivener, compile to Plain Text or MultiMarkdown with chapter titles turned on.
    Start the file at your first chapter (the Prologue or Chapter 1). Leave out the title page, copyright page and table of contents: Moss Press makes those for you.
    Put each chapter title on a line of its own.
  2. Bring it in. On the Book tab, choose how your chapters are marked under Split chapters at, then choose your file. One file for the whole book, or one file per chapter, both work.
  3. Check the chapters. Click a chapter's name on the Book tab to see and edit its title and text. If a chapter split in the wrong place, use Split chapter at cursor or Join with next chapter. For big rewrites, it's easier to edit in your writing program and import again.
  4. Set the look once. Page sets the trim size, margins, headers and page numbers. Type sets the fonts, chapter openings and scene breaks. Every chapter follows the same style automatically.
  5. Front and end matter. The title page, copyright page, dedication and contents come from the Book tab. End matter (About the Author, Also by…) and Images (author photo, covers, your own scene-break ornaments) are shared by all your books: make them once, tick them in any book, and when you edit one, every book that uses it updates.
  6. Make the PDF. Press Export PDF, choose Save as PDF, set Margins to None and turn Headers and footers off.

You can open this again any time from the Help tab.
